# Seasonal registration and returning check-in

The Registration page has two parent/guardian paths and shows only the selected form. New applications collect athlete eligibility and school details, guardian and household information, an emergency contact, and track background. Returning check-ins still ask only for guardian email and athlete first name, with no paperwork. Intake is separate from Contact, athlete accounts, the roster and Square. After SQL saves either submission, the page shows a reference the family can copy. No confirmation or decision email is sent by the application. Staff send follow-up, waitlists and offers separately from the club inbox.

## Family walkthrough

On `/registration`, the starting line offers **Apply for a spot** (`#apply`) and **Check in** (`#returning`) only when intake is open. A new guardian completes Athlete, Guardian, Household, Emergency contact and Track background, then reviews the answers before submitting. **Back** and **Edit** do not submit. Returning families have one screen and no paperwork. Drafts live only in page memory: navigating between steps retains answers, but reloading or leaving the page discards them. Do not enter real family information in a design rehearsal.

To review without opening demo or connecting to a database, run `node scripts/preview-registration.mjs` from `apps/web` after installing its dependencies. Open `http://127.0.0.1:3013/registration`; use fictional details and an address such as `family@example.test`. Practice dates and all logistics in this preview are samples. Its loopback-only fixture simulates receipts without saving submissions or sending mail. Stop with Ctrl+C. Start with the `closed` or `unavailable` argument to inspect either alternative entrance. The helper is not a launch or deployment command.

With the open preview running, `npx playwright test -c playwright.starting-line.config.ts` runs the synthetic Starting Line checks in desktop Chromium and a mobile WebKit profile. This suite intercepts submission requests. The separate SQL-backed registration rehearsal remains the deployment gate; a simulated receipt does not verify a database save.

For local photo review only, add `--archive-art` to the preview command. The helper serves only six named WebP derivatives (480, 960, and 1440 pixels wide for each of `starting-blocks` and `team-huddle`) from the checkout's ignored `artifacts/registration-archive-preview/` directory. Use metadata-stripped derivatives and never put originals or derivatives awaiting clearance in `public/`, source control, or release artifacts. The helper is loopback-only; normal development and deployments use the existing artwork. When testing this option, run the browser suite with `STARTING_LINE_ARCHIVE_PREVIEW=true`.

## Before opening

1. Deploy the reviewed immutable artifact and additive migration through the standard release workflow. Keep intake closed. Verify the canonical `/registration` route, noindex in demo, and the active Admin review workspace.
2. In **Admin → Registration → Season Settings**, enter the approved season title, November 27 opening date (Eastern), first practice date, end date, practice site/schedule, fees, reply window, paperwork inbox, in-person handoff instructions, and review and retention owners. The first practice date must be within the season and is required to open intake: athletes must turn six by that date. Saving a draft does not expose those details publicly. After editing a closed season, staff must approve it again by opening.
3. Before collecting real data, have the club approve the registration privacy notice at `/privacy#registration-privacy`, blank forms, and updated post-offer paperwork instructions. Confirm that DOB, school, household, and emergency contact details appear in private application detail only, never in queue search, telemetry, or content promotion. Confirm that no proof of age, insurance, medical information, athlete email, signatures, or attachments are collected by the online form. Do not use real families in demo. No seed seasons are shipped.
4. On Friday, November 27, confirm launch checks and manually select **Open intake**. Check both the manual switch and the effective public state in Season Settings. The API rejects new submissions before the configured Eastern date or while closed. Verify both family paths from a mobile browser on the canonical site before starting ads. **Close intake** stops new submissions without removing saved records; a form already left open in a browser cannot create a new submission after closure.

Ads can use `/registration?src=facebook` or `/registration?src=instagram`. The season totals count visits (not unique visitors), new applications, check-ins and enrollment by the three fixed channels, including Direct / other. Unknown tags become Direct. No ad pixel, tracking cookies or family data are sent to ad platforms.

## Review and paperwork

- Use **New Applications** or **Returning Check-Ins**. Search stays in the private Admin form, not the URL. **Back to queue** preserves the active filters and page; **Clear filters** resets them. Follow up, waitlist, offer, close or reopen as appropriate. Status changes never contact a family; send the decision manually.
- An offer allows paperwork while a prior-club case is unresolved. Verify any applicable AAU and USATF steps outside the app. Do not mark an athlete competition-ready until they are enrolled and transfer review is cleared or not needed.
- Only new athletes offered a spot send paperwork. Ask families to put their receipt reference in the email subject and send PDFs or clear photos to the approved inbox, or hand in the packet in person. Do not advertise texting or upload completed forms to the app. Photo consent is optional and tracked separately from enrollment. Returning athletes only complete the online check-in; staff can move them from Offered to Enrolled without collecting paperwork.
- At least 90 days after the season ends, the retention owner verifies the season is closed and asks an authorized SuperAdmin to confirm closeout in Season Settings. This permanently removes SQL submission records and linked review history while retaining channel totals. It does not delete club email, paper files, or any copies in existing backups; follow the club’s separate retention procedure for those. Before demo sign-off, use only synthetic submissions and a clearly labeled season.
